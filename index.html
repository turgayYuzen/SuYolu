1. Giriş ve Amaç

Su Yolu ("Uygulama"), Anadolu'daki kadim çeşmelerin, su kaynaklarının, vanaların ve su boru hatlarının GPS koordinatlarıyla haritalanmasını; bu verilerin çift QR kodlu mermer/pirinç tabela şablonları (CNC DXF ve Lazer SVG formatında) ile ölümsüzleştirilmesini ve olası arızalarda canlı radarla borunun tam noktasının bulunmasını sağlayan bağımsız bir mobil uygulamadır.

Summary Statement: Su Yolu does NOT transmit any personal data, user identity, or location history to external servers. All pipeline routes and critical points are stored strictly locally on your device. It is a 100% offline-ready, open field engineering tool.

1. Introduction & Overview

Su Yolu ("App") is an open-source mobile application dedicated to mapping, archiving, and locating traditional water pipelines, village fountains, valves, and natural springs using precise GPS. It provides dual-QR code marble plaque exports for CNC milling (DXF) and laser engraving (SVG), as well as a live proximity radar for excavation safety.

2. Permissions and Data Processing

Permission Purpose & Functionality Data Storage & Sharing
Precise Location
ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION
ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION		 1. Automatic coordinate recording every 10 meters while walking along water pipes.
2. Calculating real-time distance and compass bearing to the pipe and nearest valve.		 Stored locally on device only. Never transmitted to external servers or third parties.
Foreground Service
FOREGROUND_SERVICE_LOCATION		 Ensures continuous background GPS route tracking across fields while the phone is in pocket or screen locked. Used only during active recording with a visible persistent notification. User can pause at any time.
Camera
CAMERA		 Scanning the QR code etched on the fountain's plaque via ZXing barcode library. Images are processed locally in real-time. No photos or videos are stored or uploaded.
Device Storage
WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE		 Saving exported CNC (.dxf), Laser (.svg), Google Earth (.kml), and GPX files into the user's `Downloads/SuYolu` folder. Files remain strictly on user's local storage.
Network Access
INTERNET		 Loading open-source OpenStreetMap (OSM) tiles for visual route display. Standard HTTP tile requests without any identifiable personal data. Works 100% offline once cached.
